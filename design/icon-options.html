Twenty directions, plus #21, the one chosen. Each card shows the logo, then the toolbar icon at its real size (16px) and at 2×, in Chrome's light and dark toolbars. Each toolbar has four states: off, off with the open-mark badge, on (annotate mode active), and on with the badge. Brand #ff6a3d; inactive gray #6b6f76.
